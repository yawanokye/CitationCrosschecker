A full citation integrity audit, not just a citation checker

CiteIntegrity is designed to help students, supervisors, examiners, journal authors, and institutions review the quality and credibility of citations before submission.

🔎

Missing citation detection

Find in-text citations that do not appear in the reference list, including author-year inconsistencies and unmatched citations.

📚

Uncited reference detection

Identify references listed at the end of the manuscript but not cited in the body of the work.

🌐

Online reference verification

Check whether references are traceable through scholarly metadata sources and flag items needing review.

🧠

Claim-support review

Review whether cited sources appear relevant to the claims they are supporting in the manuscript.

📈

ACII integrity score

Measure overall citation health using verification integrity, concentration, author diversity, and temporal balance.

🛠️

Recovery suggestions

Generate suggestions for missing citations, unverifiable references, and sources that need closer review.

Frequently asked questions

Key points users need to understand before uploading a manuscript.

Which file format works best?

DOCX is recommended because it preserves structure better for citation and reference extraction. PDF can distort references, line breaks, and citation patterns.

Does CiteIntegrity replace academic judgement?

No. CiteIntegrity helps detect, organise, and prioritise citation issues. The final decision should still be reviewed by the author, supervisor, examiner, or editor.

What does ACII measure?

The Academic Citation Integrity Index summarises citation health using signals such as verification integrity, author diversity, citation concentration, and temporal balance.

Are uploaded documents stored permanently?

The platform is designed for secure processing and should not store manuscripts permanently. State the exact retention period in the Privacy Policy before commercial launch.

Refund guidance

Requesting a refund

For refund requests, email support with the details below so the payment can be traced quickly.

  • Email address used for payment
  • Payment provider and payment reference
  • Date of payment and amount paid
  • Job ID or analysis reference, where available
  • Brief reason for the refund request
Data and support note

Protect confidential documents

Please do not email full confidential manuscripts unless you are authorised to do so. For technical support, send the job ID, browser, file type and a brief description of the issue.

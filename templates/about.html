Who we are

CiteIntegrity is owned by Anovlad Technologies.

CiteIntegrity is a specialised academic technology platform developed to support citation integrity, reference verification, source recovery and manuscript quality review.

The platform is built for real academic workflows where citation errors, missing references, uncited sources and unsupported claims can affect research quality, supervisor review, journal submission and institutional credibility.

Our goal is simple: help users detect citation problems early, review evidence more carefully and submit work with greater confidence.

What we do

Practical citation integrity tools for academic users.

  • Match in-text citations against reference lists.
  • Identify missing references and uncited references.
  • Verify references through online scholarly sources where available.
  • Support recovery of unresolved or incomplete sources.
  • Review claim-support signals around cited evidence.
  • Generate citation integrity reports and ACII score summaries.
Our focus

Designed for academic quality, not text rewriting.

🔍

Integrity checking

We focus on finding citation gaps, reference mismatches and unresolved sources before they affect academic review.

📚

Evidence awareness

CiteIntegrity encourages users to examine whether sources are traceable, relevant and sufficiently connected to the manuscript.

📄

Submission readiness

Reports and review outputs help users improve citation quality before thesis, dissertation, project or journal submission.

Our trust position

Your work remains yours.

CiteIntegrity is designed as a citation and reference review tool. It does not replace the researcher, supervisor, examiner or editor. It helps users identify issues that require human review and academic judgement.

Responsible use principles

  • Review-only guidance, not automatic academic judgement.
  • No rewriting of the user’s manuscript as a substitute for authorship.
  • No submission of documents to plagiarism databases.
  • No use of uploaded documents for public indexing or model training.
  • Clear reporting to support human decision-making.
